# Variomes

Variomes is a high-recall search engine that ranks the literature about a genomic variant and prioritizes sets of variants, to support variant curation and evidence-based decisions in precision oncology.

## What it does

Precision oncology uses treatments that target specific genetic variants. Finding the clinically actionable variants of a patient, and the evidence about them, means searching the literature for many variants, and variants have no universally adopted nomenclature. Variomes ranks the publications about a variant to support evidence-based decisions. It can also prioritize the variants of a Variant Call Format (VCF) file, assuming that the clinical actionability of a variant correlates with the volume of literature about it.

Variomes searches four pre-annotated collections: MEDLINE abstracts, PubMed Central full-text articles, ClinicalTrials.gov records and supplementary materials. The 2022 paper describes the first three [@pasche2022variomes]; supplementary materials were added later [@pasche2023supplementary]. Variomes expands each variant with the synonyms generated by [SynVar](../synvar/index.md) to retrieve more documents, then ranks them with several strategies.

In the published evaluation, almost two-thirds of the top five publications were relevant for clinical decision support, and 81.8% of clinically actionable variants were ranked in the top three. On 803 queries, Variomes retrieved on average 21.3% more articles than LitVar [@pasche2022variomes].

## Using Variomes

The web application offers three types of query: search the literature for one or several variants, prioritize a set of variants uploaded as a file (one gene and variant per line), or reload previous results with their query identifier. A query can be refined with patient information (diagnosis, gender, age) and with advanced options: publication dates (the last 10 years by default), number of documents per collection, the elements that must be mentioned in a publication, and query expansion with synonyms of genes, variants and diseases. Results are shown per collection, with the passages that mention the variant. The [tutorial](https://variomes.sibils.org/tutorial) on the Variomes site explains each option.

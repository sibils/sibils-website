# Publications

Papers, conference abstracts, datasets and reports about SIBiLS and its services, and published work that uses them. Each entry links to its DOI and, when the work is indexed there, to PubMed.
{ .lead }

## How to cite

!!! cite ""

    If you use SIBiLS, its APIs or one of its web applications, please cite both papers:

    - Gobeill J, Caucheteur D, Michel PA, Mottin L, Pasche E, Ruch P (2020). SIB Literature Services: RESTful customizable search engines in biomedical literature, enriched with automatically mapped biomedical concepts. *Nucleic Acids Research* 48(W1):W12–W16. [doi:10.1093/nar/gkaa328](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkaa328) · [PMID 32379317](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32379317/)
    - Caucheteur D, Flament A, Gobeill J, Pasche E, Michel PA, Mottin L, Liyanapathirana J, Mottaz A, Ruch P (2026). Enhancing the SIB Literature Services (SIBiLS) with annotations to support biocuration. *Database* 2026:baag034. [doi:10.1093/database/baag034](https://doi.org/10.1093/database/baag034) · [PMID 42768721](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42768721/)

When you use a service or a collection that has its own publication, please cite it as well:

| Service or data | Publication |
|-----------------|-------------|
| BiodiversityPMC | Pasche et al. (2023), [Towards “Biodiversity PMC”](https://doi.org/10.3897/biss.7.111647) and [From SIBiLS to Biodiversity PMC](https://doi.org/10.3897/biss.7.111660) |
| Supplementary data | Gobeill et al. (2025), [Unlocking the potential of PubMed Central supplementary data files](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioadv/vbaf155) |
| Variomes | Pasche et al. (2022), [Variomes: a high recall search engine to support the curation of genomic variants](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btac146) |
| SynVar | Mottaz et al. (2022), [Designing an optimal expansion method to improve the recall of a genomic variant curation-support service](https://doi.org/10.3233/SHTI220603), with the Variomes paper |
| BiotXplorer | Ruch et al. (2024), [BiotXplorer: navigating evidence-based biotic interactions](https://doi.org/10.3897/biss.8.135453) |
| DisTriage | Nugnes et al. (2026), [DisProt in 2026](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkaf1175) |
| COVoc vocabulary | Caucheteur et al. (2023), [COVoc and COVTriage: novel resources to support literature triage](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btac800) |
| Question-Answering, SPARQL endpoint, CellTriage | No dedicated publication: cite the two SIBiLS papers above |

## Work using SIBiLS

Published work that uses SIBiLS or one of its services, or reports its use, by other groups and in evaluation campaigns. The second line of each entry says how SIBiLS was used.

<div class="biblio" markdown>

- Camperio G, Zemp C, Downes J, Wildpret W, Borges PAV, Agosti D, et al. (2026). Synthesizing evidence on island biodiversity monitoring: from scattered literature to structured knowledge. World Biodiversity Forum 2026, Davos, Switzerland, 14–19 June 2026, abstract WBF2026-686. [doi:10.5194/wbf2026-686](https://doi.org/10.5194/wbf2026-686)<br>About 700 publications on island biodiversity monitoring were deposited in BiodiversityPMC through the Biodiversity Literature Repository for annotation and data extraction.

DisProt in 2026: enhancing intrinsically disordered proteins accessibility, deposition, and annotation. *Nucleic Acids Research* 54(D1):D383–D392. [doi:10.1093/nar/gkaf1175](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkaf1175) · [PMID 41249866](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41249866/)<br>Presents DisTriage, a weekly literature triage for DisProt curators built on SIBiLS; its training texts are fetched with the SIBiLS Fetch API.
- Wood V, Jeffryes M, Green AF, Blum M, Orchard S, Panni S, et al. (2026). Empowering biological knowledgebases: advances in human-in-the-loop AI-driven literature curation. *Bioinformatics Advances* 6(1):vbag028. Gender and geographical bias in the editorial decision-making process of biomedical journals: a case-control study. *BMJ Evidence-Based Medicine* 30(3):149–162. [doi:10.1136/bmjebm-2024-113083](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjebm-2024-113083) · [PMID 39721743](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39721743/)<br>Study attributes of 6,213 submitted manuscripts were extracted with machine learning built on SIBiLS.
- Elshishka M, Mladenov A, Altash S, Álvarez-Ortega S, Peña-Santiago R, Peneva V (2024). [doi:10.3897/BDJ.12.e126315](https://doi.org/10.3897/BDJ.12.e126315) · [PMID 39346622](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39346622/)<br>Links each species of the catalogue to SIBiLS search results in the Plazi collection.

[doi:10.1093/database/baae034](https://doi.org/10.1093/database/baae034) · [PMID 38752292](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38752292/)<br>Uses SynVar to place variants on the canonical reference sequence.
- Nachtegael C, Gravel B, Dillen A, Smits G, Nowé A, Papadimitriou S, Lenaerts T (2022). Scaling up oligogenic diseases research with OLIDA: the Oligogenic Diseases Database. *Database* 2022:baac023. - Pasche E, Caucheteur D, Mottin L, Mottaz A, Gobeill J, Ruch P (2020). [doi:10.6028/NIST.SP.1266.pm-BITEM](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.SP.1266.pm-BITEM)<br>Queries the SIBiLS MEDLINE index with keywords and with the normalised concept identifiers of SIBiLS annotations.
- Caucheteur D, Pasche E, Gobeill J, Mottaz A, Mottin L, Ruch P (2019).
